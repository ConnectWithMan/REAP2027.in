Age Limit Norms

As per AICTE and CEG guidelines, there is no upper or lower age limit for registering and participating in REAP 2027 admissions. Candidates of any age who satisfy the minimum academic qualifications are fully eligible.

Medical Fitness Standard

Every candidate must be physically and mentally fit to undergo rigorous engineering coursework. Candidates must submit a Medical Fitness Certificate signed by a registered Government Medical Officer at the time of final institute reporting.

Domicile Hierarchy (85% vs 15%)

85% seats are strictly reserved for bona-fide residents of Rajasthan. 15% seats are open for Outside Rajasthan (OR) candidates across all states and union territories of India based on JEE Main CRL Rank.

Step-by-Step REAP 2027 Registration & Counselling Journey

Comprehensive walkthrough of the sequential admission process from online form submission to physical college reporting.

01
Qualify JEE Main 2027 or 12th Board PCM / DiplomaStage 1

Secure your JEE Main 2027 score card (for Priority 1 admissions) or secure 45%+ (40% for reserved category) in Class 12th Science PCM / 3-Year Diploma. Candidates aiming for B.Arch must obtain valid NATA 2027 or JEE Paper 2 scores.

02
Online Portal Registration & ₹885 Fee PaymentStage 2

Visit the official REAP 2027 admission portal (ceg.rajasthan.gov.in / reap2027.com). Enter basic identity details, select your eligibility stream (Priority 1, 2, or 3), and pay the non-refundable registration fee of ₹885/- online or via e-Mitra.

03
Candidate Profile Filling & Document UploadsStage 3

Log into your candidate account using the generated Form Number and password. Fill personal, category, domicile, and academic score data. Upload scanned copies of candidate photograph, signature, 10th marksheet, 12th/Diploma marksheet, and category/income certificates.

04
Provisional Merit List & Grievance RedressalStage 4

CEG Rajasthan publishes separate provisional merit lists for JEE Main (Priority 1), Class 12th Board (Priority 2), and Diploma (Priority 3). Candidates verify their rank, category status, and domicile data. Submit corrections/objections during the official grievance window.

05
Online Choice Filling & Choice LockingStage 5

Open the choice filling panel to enter preferred combinations of colleges and engineering branches in descending order of true preference. Fill maximum available choices. Save frequently and lock your choices using OTP verification before the deadline.

06
Seat Allotment Result & Provisional Allotment LetterStage 6

CEG runs the automated centralized allotment algorithm. Check allotment status on the REAP portal. If allotted a seat, download the official Provisional Seat Allotment Letter indicating institute name, branch, reporting dates, and fee instructions.

07
Physical Institute Reporting & Upward MovementStage 7

Mandatory Step: Report in person to the allotted engineering college with original documents, 2 sets of self-attested photocopies, and semester admission fees. Candidates who report and wish to upgrade to a higher preference branch can opt for Upward Movement.

OBC-NCL & MBC-NCL Certificate Validity Rules (Rajasthan DoP)

As per Government of Rajasthan Department of Personnel orders, an OBC/MBC Non-Creamy Layer certificate is valid for one year from date of issuance. If issued within the last 3 years, it can be validated for REAP counselling by attaching a notarized declaration in Format-D stating that the non-creamy layer status remains unchanged. Creamy layer candidates are treated under the General (Unreserved) category.

Women Horizontal Reservation (25%)

In accordance with Government of Rajasthan guidelines, 25% of seats in each category (General, SC, ST, OBC, MBC, EWS) are horizontally reserved for female candidates across all participating engineering colleges.

Defence & Ex-Servicemen Quota

Wards of defence personnel and ex-servicemen are allotted seats under horizontal reservation classified into Priorities I through IX as defined by the Ministry of Defence, Government of India (Sainik Kalyan Board certificate required).

TFWS Seat Nature

TFWS seats are supernumerary (5% over and above the sanctioned intake). Candidates admitted under TFWS pay zero tuition fees for all 4 years. Only examination, development, and caution deposit fees apply.

Common Mistakes to Avoid During REAP 2027 Registration

Critical precautions to ensure your registration is error-free and your candidature is not rejected during physical verification.

Caution 1

Name & DOB Mismatch

Entering candidate or parental names differently from the Class 10th Secondary Board Certificate. Discrepancies cause rejection during college reporting.

Remedy: Enter data exactly matching your 10th mark sheet word-for-word.
Caution 2

Wrong Priority Selection

Candidates with a valid JEE Main 2027 rank mistakenly applying under Priority 2 (12th Board PCM) lose the top priority advantage during seat allotment.

Remedy: Always choose Priority 1 if you appeared for JEE Main 2027.
Caution 3

Outdated Category Certificates

Uploading expired OBC-NCL or MBC-NCL certificates without the required valid declaration leads to automatic reclassification into General (Unreserved).

Remedy: Ensure digital certificates comply with the Rajasthan DoP validity timeline.
Caution 4

Insufficient Choices Filled

Filling only 2 or 3 college-branch choices based on high expectations. If cutoff ranks rise, the candidate ends up with no seat allotment.

Remedy: Fill 30 to 50+ choices in true preference order to maximize allotment chances.
Caution 5

Skipping Physical Reporting

Believing that online seat allotment alone confirms admission. Failing to report in-person at the allotted institute results in immediate cancellation of candidature.

Remedy: Report in person within the deadline with original documents and fees.
Caution 6

Missing TFWS Income Format-D

Opting for TFWS tuition waiver without obtaining the statutory Format-D income certificate signed by an authorized Tehsildar.

Remedy: Get the Format-D income certificate certified by your local Tehsil before reporting.
