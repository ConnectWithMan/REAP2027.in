Summary for Students & Parents: reap2027.in is an independent information guide. We do not ask for or store passwords, Aadhaar cards, credit card numbers, or UPI credentials. Official fee payments (₹885) and registration occur exclusively on the CEG portal.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal personal data necessary to provide a smooth, responsive, and secure experience for our users:

  • Voluntarily Provided Information: When you submit a student query through our Contact page, we collect your name, email address, contact telephone number, and your question details to provide personalized educational guidance.
  • Automatically Collected Technical Data: When you navigate our pages, our servers automatically log standard diagnostic data including your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser model, device type, operating system, pages visited, timestamps, and referring URLs.
  • Push Notification Tokens: If you explicitly consent to browser push alerts via OneSignal, a pseudonymized push subscription token is generated by your browser to send admission dates and cutoff updates.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is utilized solely for lawful educational purposes, including:

  • Delivering accurate cutoff data, seat matrix calculators, and counselling guides.
  • Responding directly to inquiries and support requests sent through our contact forms.
  • Analyzing aggregated site usage trends to improve page responsiveness and mobile performance.
  • Detecting, preventing, and addressing technical glitches, cybersecurity threats, or unauthorized access.

Zero Commercial Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your contact details to coaching institutes, private lead-aggregators, or telemarketers under any circumstance.

3. Cookies, Google AdSense & Analytics

Our website utilizes standard web cookies (small text files stored on your browser) to ensure seamless functionality:

  • Google Analytics (GA4 - G-BSNSPWXM02): We use Google Analytics to measure aggregate site performance, such as most frequented cutoff archives and average read times. Data collected is anonymous and masked.
  • Google AdSense (ca-pub-9724513291347385): We partner with Google AdSense to display advertisements that support the hosting and maintenance of this free portal. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Personalized Advertising Opt-Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Web Push Notifications (OneSignal)

We integrate OneSignal Web SDK to notify candidates regarding critical REAP 2027 milestones (e.g. registration opening, choice locking deadlines, seat allotment announcements). Push notifications are strictly opt-in. You can revoke or disable notification permissions at any time via your browser settings (Site Settings → Notifications → Block).

5. Your Rights Under Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023

Under the provisions of India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, you hold the following rights regarding personal data you have shared with us:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of any personal data you have submitted to us via forms.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: You may request the modification or immediate permanent deletion of your inquiry messages and contact details from our records.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: You can contact our designated Grievance Officer regarding any data processing concern.
  • Right to Nominate: You may designate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.

6. Data Security & Storage

We deploy robust organizational and technological safeguards, including standard 256-bit SSL encryption (HTTPS), firewalls, and strict server access controls to prevent unauthorized data loss, alteration, or exposure. While no digital platform can guarantee 100% invulnerability, we continually review and modernize our protocols.

7. External Portals & References

Our website references official state portals (such as ceg.rajasthan.gov.in) and affiliating universities for educational attribution and informational context. Please note that external websites operate independently under their own privacy statements and terms of use.

8. Children's Privacy

Our educational resources are designed for prospective engineering students typically aged 16 and older who have completed or are appearing for senior secondary school (Class 12th) examinations. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable data from minors under 13 without parental consent.
