How to Check & Download REAP 2027 Seat Allotment Letter

Step-by-step authoritative walkthrough on checking your provisional allotment order on the official portal, verifying critical parameters, and exercising post-allotment options.

4-Step Online Download Walkthrough

Step 1 Access the Official Portal

Navigate directly to the official admission portal. Look for the active link: "REAP 2027 Seat Allotment Result / Provisional Allotment Letter".

Step 2 Authenticate with Candidate Credentials

Enter your REAP 2027 Form / Application Number (generated during registration) and your registered password or Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY) along with the security captcha code.

Step 3 View Allotted College, Branch & Quota

The dashboard reveals your provisional allotment status: the matched college name, branch, seat type (GAS, SFS, or TFWS), matched choice serial number, and allotted category quota.

Step 4 Download Allotment Order & Fee Challan

Click "Print Provisional Allotment Letter". Download the official PDF document which includes your reporting instructions, nodal center address, and provisional acceptance fee challan. Print at least 3 clear copies.

Anatomy of the Official Allotment Letter

The provisional seat allotment order is a legal document issued by CEG Rajasthan containing 5 critical verified parameters:
1. Candidate & Merit Identification
Candidate Name, Father's Name, REAP Application ID, and Merit Priority (Priority 1 JEE CRL or Priority 2 12th PCM %).
2. Allotted Institution & Affiliation Code
Official college name (e.g. MBM University Jodhpur, CTAE Udaipur, SKIT) along with CEG institute code and affiliating university.
3. Engineering Specialization & Seat Type
Approved branch name (e.g. CSE, AI-DS, ECE) and precise seat classification: GAS (Govt. Aided), SFS (Self-Financed), or TFWS (Tuition Waiver).
4. Category Quota & Choice Preference Number
The exact quota under which the seat is allocated (e.g. OBC-NCL Rajasthan Domicile) and your preference order rank (e.g. Choice #3).
5. Physical Reporting Window & Fee Details
Reporting deadline dates, provisional acceptance fee amount, and contact information for the institute scrutiny officer.

Official Seat Cascading & De-Reservation Protocol

Under CEG allotment norms, if sub-category seats (such as Female, PwD, or Ex-Servicemen) remain vacant at the end of initial evaluation, they automatically merge back into the open category of that specific community (e.g., vacant OBC-Female seats merge into OBC-General). Furthermore, any unfilled Outside Rajasthan (OR 15%) seats from Round 1 are cascaded directly into the Rajasthan State Domicile General open pool for Round 2.

The REAP Seat Allotment Algorithm Explained

How does the computer allocate your seat? Learn the deterministic four-step mathematical logic used by CEG Jaipur to process hundreds of thousands of candidate choices.

1. Tier Hierarchy

The allocation engine groups candidates into 3 distinct priorities. Priority 1 candidates (JEE Main CRL) are allocated first until completely exhausted. Priority 2 candidates (12th Board PCM %) are evaluated only thereafter.

P1 > P2 > P3 Strict Tiering

2. Top-Down Choice Scan

When your turn arrives in the merit order, the system scans your choices strictly from Choice #1 downward. The very first branch-college choice with an eligible vacant seat matching your quota is locked.

Sequential Scanning

3. Quota & Category Match

The engine checks open unreserved seats first. If an open seat is unavailable, your reserved category quota (OBC-NCL, EWS, SC, ST, MBC, TFWS) is checked. Horizontal sub-quotas (Female 25%, PwD 5%) are integrated simultaneously.

Dual-Layer Reservation

4. Tie-Breaking Resolution

If two candidates share identical merit marks, ties are broken via: (1) Higher Mathematics percentile/marks, (2) Higher Physics percentile/marks, (3) Higher Chemistry marks, and (4) Older candidate age as per 10th marksheet.

Maths > Phys > Chem > Age
Crucial Insight: Why You Must Never Compromise Choice Order

Because the engine locks the first available match from the top, any lower choice you place above a better choice will permanently block you from getting the better choice. Never place a "safe" college above your dream college. If your rank qualifies for both Choice #1 and Choice #5, you will ALWAYS be allotted Choice #1!

Upward Movement Rules & Zero-Risk Upgradation

Upward Movement is Rajasthan's unique safeguard for engineering aspirants. Understand how you can safely target higher-preference colleges without jeopardizing your confirmed seat.

Scenario A: You Are Upgraded

Higher-Preference Seat Found
  • The engine assigns you a higher-ranked choice from your original choice list (e.g., from Choice #7 to Choice #3).
  • Your previous seat (Choice #7) is instantly and automatically cancelled and released to other merit candidates.
  • You download a revised Provisional Allotment Order and report to the new institute.
  • Your provisional fee deposited at the initial college is officially transferred to the new college as per CEG regulations.

Scenario B: You Are NOT Upgraded

No Higher Vacancy Available
  • No eligible vacancy opened in choices #1 through #6 above your current allotment.
  • Your original allotted seat (Choice #7) remains 100% intact and confirmed.
  • You CANNOT lose your existing seat because you applied for upward movement.
  • You continue your studies in the currently reported college without any disruption or penalty.
Mandatory Condition to Qualify for Upward Movement

To be eligible for Upward Movement, you MUST complete online reporting at the allotted institute in Round 1 or Round 2, verify your original documents, and pay the provisional acceptance fee. If you skip online reporting, your allotment is cancelled immediately, and you are disqualified from Upward Movement.

5-Step Physical Reporting Roadmap

Log in to the official admission portal immediately upon round result declaration. Download the provisional seat allotment letter and keep 3 printed copies handy.

Step 2 Deposit Provisional Acceptance Fee

Pay the mandatory provisional admission fee online via payment gateway or at authorized e-Mitra kiosks before heading to the college.

Step 3 In-Person Reporting at Nodal College

Visit the allotted college campus during designated reporting hours (9:00 AM - 4:00 PM). Ensure the candidate is physically present with a parent/guardian.

Present original certificates to the institute scrutiny committee. Verification officers cross-check marks, domicile, category status, and medical fitness.

Step 5 Confirmation Receipt & Upward Consent

Receive the stamped official Admission Confirmation Slip. If applying for Upward Movement, complete the online consent check at the college nodal terminal.
