Physical Institute Reporting Verification Dossier

Upon seat allotment in Round 1, Round 2, or Internal Sliding, you must report in-person to your allotted college with original certificates and two complete self-attested photocopy sets.

1. Academic Dossier

Mark sheets, qualifying proofs & school certificates

  • Class 10th (Secondary) Marksheet & Certificate Mandatory legal proof of Date of Birth and parentage spelling.
  • Class 12th (Senior Secondary) Marksheet & Passing Certificate Verification of 45% aggregate in Physics + Mathematics + 1 Optional Subject (40% for reserved RJ candidates).
  • Diploma Consolidated Marksheets & Certificate (if applicable) All semester marksheets and AICTE-recognized diploma completion proof for Priority 3 applicants.
  • JEE Main 2027 Scorecard / NATA 2027 Scorecard Printed official scorecard showing CRL rank, category rank, and examination roll number.
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) & Character Certificate (CC) Original copies issued by the Head/Principal of the school/college last attended.
  • Migration Certificate Mandatory for candidates passing from CBSE, ICSE, NIOS, or other state boards outside RBSE Rajasthan.

2. Domicile & Identity Dossier

State residence status & government ID cards

  • Rajasthan Bonafide Certificate (Mool Niwas Praman Patra) Mandatory for 85% Rajasthan state quota. Must have digital signature & e-Mitra verification barcode.
  • Government Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card) Original Aadhaar card, Voter ID, or Indian Passport along with self-attested photocopies.
  • 6 to 8 Recent Passport-Size Photographs Identical color photographs matching the one uploaded during online registration.
  • Outside Rajasthan (OR) Address & Nativity Proof Mandatory for 15% OR quota candidates (Aadhaar or native state domicile certificate).
  • Parent Service Certificate (for Domicile Clauses) Required if claiming Rajasthan domicile under Clause B, C, or D (Rajasthan / Central Govt employee posting).

3. Category & Reservation Dossier

Caste, economic & special quota credentials

  • SC / ST Category Certificate Issued by Tehsildar/SDO in Rajasthan. Lifetime validity with recognizable caste notification.
  • OBC-NCL / MBC-NCL Certificate (with Form-E if needed) Valid for 1 year. If 1–3 years old, must be accompanied by Form-E ₹50 stamp paper affidavit.
  • GEN-EWS Income & Asset Certificate Valid for FY 2026-2027 / 2027-2028 issued by SDO/Tehsildar complying with income & residential asset caps.
  • PwD / Divyangjan Disability Certificate Minimum 40% permanent disability issued by District Medical Board / UDID card.
  • Ex-Servicemen / Defence Certificate (WDP) Certificate issued by Zila Sainik Kalyan Adhikari clearly stating Defence Priority I through VIII.
  • Kashmiri Migrant / Kashmiri Pandit Certificate Issued by competent Relief Commissioner / District Magistrate for KM supernumerary seats.

4. REAP Portal & Financial Dossier

Allotment letters, receipts & semester fees

  • REAP 2027 Online Application Form Printout Downloaded confirmation copy of candidate registration form duly signed with pasted photograph.
  • Provisional Seat Allotment Letter Downloaded from the REAP candidate portal showing allotted college, branch, and category.
  • ₹885 Registration Fee Transaction Receipt Bank challan, online gateway transaction slip, or e-Mitra counter payment receipt.
  • TFWS Income Certificate (Format I / II / III) Mandatory for candidates allotted 100% tuition fee waiver seats (total income < ₹8 Lakhs).
  • College Admission / First Semester Fee Instrument Demand Draft (DD) or online payment receipt as specified in your college's reporting instructions.

5. Affidavits & Medical Proformas

Mandatory sworn undertakings & health checks

  • Medical Fitness Certificate (Official REAP Format) Signed & stamped by a registered Government Medical Officer (MBBS minimum) verifying height, vision & health.
  • Gap Year Affidavit (Notarized on Stamp Paper) Required if passing 12th/Diploma in 2026 or prior. Stating candidate was preparing for competitive exams on ₹50/100 stamp.
  • Anti-Ragging Undertaking (Student & Parent) Affidavit or online registration reference from antiragging.in as per Supreme Court / UGC guidelines.
  • Form-E OBC Non-Creamy Layer Affidavit (if applicable) Required if OBC/MBC certificate was issued between 1 and 3 years prior to admission.
  • Upward Movement Option / Retention Undertaking Submitted at the reporting counter if candidate wants to participate in subsequent upward movement rounds.

6. Dossier Assembly Guidelines

How to arrange your documents for rapid clearance

  • File Folder 1: Original Documents Arranged strictly in sequence (Allotment Letter > 10th > 12th > Domicile > Category > Medical). Handed over for physical inspection.
  • File Folder 2: Self-Attested Set A Complete set of photocopies with candidate's original signature & date on every single page. Retained by institute registrar.
  • File Folder 3: Self-Attested Set B Second identical photocopy set retained by college student welfare / scholarship department.
  • Digital Backup on Cloud / Pen Drive Keep high-resolution PDF scans of all documents on Google Drive or Digilocker for emergency re-prints.

Step-by-Step Document Verification Workflow

When reporting to premier institutions like MBM Jodhpur, CTAE Udaipur, or RTU Kota, candidates proceed through 5 structured verification counters before provisional admission is finalized.

1

Counter 1: Token & Identity

Candidate submits REAP Seat Allotment Letter and Aadhaar Card. Biometric verification or photo-match is conducted and a verification token number is allotted.

2

Counter 2: Academic Scrutiny

Faculty verification officer cross-checks 10th DOB proof, 12th PCM / Diploma marksheets, aggregate percentages (45%/40%), and JEE Main 2027 rank cards.

3

Counter 3: Domicile & Caste

Revenue scrutiny officer verifies Rajasthan Bonafide digital barcode, OBC/MBC 1-year validity + Form-E affidavit, EWS year stamp, and TFWS income proofs.

4

Counter 4: Fee Deposit

Candidate deposits college semester fees via Demand Draft, Net Banking, or college POS machine, and collects the official stamped college fee receipt.

5

Counter 5: Final Admission Slip

Reporting officer issues the stamped Provisional Admission Confirmation Slip and accepts your Upward Movement participation option form.

Top 8 Document Disqualification Traps & Remedies

Every year, hundreds of qualified candidates lose allotted government engineering seats due to preventable documentation flaws. Review these common pitfalls and their immediate legal solutions.

OBC / MBC Certificate Expired

Submitting an OBC-NCL certificate issued more than 1 year prior to the REAP registration date without an accompanying Form-E affidavit. Candidate is converted to General category and loses reserved seat.

Official Remedy: If certificate is between 1 and 3 years old, execute the prescribed Form-E Non-Creamy Layer Affidavit on ₹50 stamp paper before reporting day.

Spelling Mismatch in Candidate / Parents' Name

Variation in name spelling between Class 10th marksheet, Aadhaar card, and caste certificate (e.g., "Kumar" missing, initials expanded or shortened).

Official Remedy: Execute a Notarized Name Discrepancy Affidavit on ₹50 non-judicial stamp paper stating that both names refer to the same individual.

Missing Digital Barcode on Domicile (Mool Niwas)

Submitting hand-written or obsolete manual bonafide certificates from 10+ years ago lacking the government e-Mitra digital barcode and QR code.

Official Remedy: Apply at any local e-Mitra kiosk for a digitally signed Rajasthan Bonafide Certificate well in advance of counselling.

EWS Certificate for Wrong Financial Year

Submitting an EWS certificate assessed on earlier financial years. EWS certificates are valid strictly for the financial year of admission (FY 2026-2027 / 2027-2028).

Official Remedy: Obtain a fresh EWS certificate from your Tehsildar / SDO issued after April 1st of the admission cycle.

Failure to Produce Migration Certificate

Candidates from CBSE, ICSE, NIOS, or outside Rajasthan boards failing to bring the original Migration Certificate issued by their respective board.

Official Remedy: Download digital migration from DigiLocker or request an urgent provisional undertaking from the college granting 14 days to submit.

No Gap Certificate for Dropper Candidates

Candidates who passed Class 12th in 2026, 2025, or earlier reporting without a notarized gap affidavit explaining what they did during the intervening period.

Official Remedy: Get a simple Gap Year Affidavit notarized on ₹50 or ₹100 stamp paper stating you were preparing for competitive engineering exams.

TFWS Income Certificate on Unofficial Plain Paper

Submitting self-declarations or employer letters without Tehsildar authentication or without using REAP Formats I, II, or III. Result: TFWS seat is cancelled.

Official Remedy: Use strictly the official REAP 2027 Income Certificate proforma and get it signed by an Executive Magistrate or Tehsildar.

Missing Medical Certificate Registration Number

Medical Fitness Certificates issued by private clinics without doctor's state medical council registration number or government hospital seal.

Official Remedy: Visit a nearby Government Primary/Community Health Centre (PHC/CHC) or District Hospital to get the REAP proforma signed by a Govt. Medical Officer.
