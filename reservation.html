Home State Quota (85%) vs Outside Rajasthan Quota (15%)

Every government, university, aided, and private engineering college in Rajasthan reserves seats under a two-tier jurisdiction framework governed by domicile norms.

85% Allotted

Rajasthan Home State Quota (85%)

Reserved exclusively for candidates possessing a valid Rajasthan state Bonafide / Domicile certificate under Clause A, B, C, or D.

  • Full Category Benefits: All vertical reservations (SC, ST, OBC, MBC, EWS) and horizontal sub-quotas apply.
  • Relaxed Academic Cutoff: Reserved category candidates need 40.00% aggregate in 10+2 PCM (compared to 45% for General).
  • AICTE TFWS Access: Eligible to opt for 5% supernumerary TFWS seats offering 100% tuition waiver.
  • Merit Hierarchy: Priority 1 (JEE Main AIR), Priority 2 (12th Board Percentile), Priority 3 (Diploma).

Allotment Round

Allotted in Round 2 (State JEE Merit) and Round 3 (State 12th PCM Percentile Merit).

15% All India

Outside Rajasthan Quota (15%)

Open to all Indian nationals who passed Class 12th outside Rajasthan or do not hold a Rajasthan Bonafide / Domicile certificate.

  • General Merit Only: Caste-based reservations (SC, ST, OBC, EWS) from other states do NOT apply in Rajasthan colleges.
  • Strict Academic Cutoff: Must have minimum 45.00% aggregate marks in 10+2 PCM with no rounding off.
  • No TFWS Concession: TFWS seats are strictly not available for Outside Rajasthan applicants.
  • Exclusively JEE Main: Allotted strictly on the basis of JEE Main 2027 All India CRL Rank (Round 1).

First Counselling Round

Round 1 of REAP counselling is conducted exclusively for Outside Rajasthan (OR) candidates.

21% Reservation

OBC-NCL (21%) Rules

Candidate must belong to Rajasthan state OBC list and fall strictly under the Non-Creamy Layer (annual family income < ₹8 Lakhs).

Affidavit Rule: If issued between 1 and 3 years ago, submit a notarized Form-E Non-Creamy Layer declaration.

Central vs State List: State Rajasthan OBC certificate is required. Central OBC certificates alone are not accepted if caste is not in state list.

10% Reservation

EWS (10%) Rules

Applicable to General category candidates whose total family gross annual income is below ₹8.00 Lakhs for the preceding financial year.

Asset Exclusions: Candidates owning agricultural land of 5 acres or more, or residential flat ≥ 1000 sq ft do not qualify.

Validity: The EWS certificate must be issued by SDO / Tehsildar and clearly state validity for FY 2026-2027.

5% Reservation

MBC-NCL (5%) Rules

Specifically enacted by the Rajasthan government for Gujjar, Raika/Rebari, Gadia Lohar, Banjara, and Gadariya communities.

Non-Creamy Layer: Similar to OBC, candidate must be non-creamy layer with valid digital certificate.

De-reservation: If MBC seats remain vacant after internal sliding, they convert to OBC seats before spot rounds.

Horizontal Reservation Policies

Horizontal reservation cuts across all vertical categories (GEN, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, MBC). Candidates allotted under horizontal quota consume a seat within their parent vertical category.

25% Reserved

Women Candidates (25%)

A minimum of 25% seats in each vertical category are reserved strictly for female candidates (Gen-F, OBC-F, SC-F, ST-F, EWS-F, MBC-F).

  • Significantly lower closing ranks in government engineering colleges (MBM, CTAE, RTU).
  • If qualified female candidates are not available, vacant seats revert to male candidates of the exact same category.
5% Reserved

Persons with Disability (5%)

5% horizontal reservation for candidates with permanent physical disability (Divyangjan) of 40% or more.

  • Must produce disability certificate issued by an authorized Government Medical Board / Unique Disability ID (UDID).
  • Candidates must be certified physically fit to undergo standard engineering laboratory curricula.
3% Reserved

Defense & Ex-Servicemen (3%)

3% horizontal reservation reserved for children of defense personnel and ex-servicemen of Rajasthan domicile.

  • Allotted strictly in order of the Ministry of Defence 9-Tier Priority hierarchy.
  • Discharge book & Zila Sainik Kalyan Board certificate required during reporting.

AICTE Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS)

Up to 5% supernumerary seats in all AICTE-approved government and private engineering colleges are allocated to meritorious students with zero tuition fees.

100% Tuition Waived

Key TFWS Entitlements & Rules

  • Supernumerary Nature: TFWS seats are created over and above the sanctioned intake (e.g. 3 extra seats for an intake of 60).
  • Complete Tuition Exemption: 100% tuition fees are waived for all 4 years of the B.Tech program. Students pay only nominal college development, exam, and caution deposits.
  • Merit Allocation: Allotted strictly in order of REAP 2027 merit ranks among applicants who opted for TFWS seat codes.
  • Branch Non-Transferable: Students admitted under TFWS cannot request internal branch change in higher semesters.

Annual Savings

Students save between ₹60,000 to ₹1,20,000 annually in government and private institutions.

Mandatory Eligibility

TFWS Qualification Criteria

  • Income Threshold: Gross annual family income from all sources must be strictly less than ₹8,00,000/-.
  • Rajasthan Bonafide: Candidate must possess a valid Rajasthan Bonafide / Domicile certificate. Outside Rajasthan students are not eligible.
  • Income Certificate: Form 16 / Tehsildar-signed Income Certificate (Format I, II, or III) issued within the current financial year.
  • Separate Choice Codes: During choice filling, select branch codes suffixed with 'T' (e.g., 101-CS-T for Computer Science TFWS).

Choice Filling Alert

You must fill regular branch codes as backup in case your rank falls short of competitive TFWS cutoffs.

Rajasthan Domicile Eligibility Norms (Clauses A, B, C, D)

To claim benefits under the 85% Home State Quota and state caste reservations, candidates must satisfy at least one of the official domicile eligibility clauses recognized by the Government of Rajasthan.

Clause A

Bonafide Resident by Birth

Candidate or either of their natural parents is a bonafide resident of Rajasthan, possessing a digital Mool Niwas certificate issued by the competent revenue authority.

Clause B

3-Year Continuous Study

Candidate who studied continuously for at least 3 years in recognized educational institutions in Rajasthan and passed the qualifying 10+2 examination from a Rajasthan board/school.

Clause C

State Govt / PSU Employees

Sons and daughters of serving or retired employees of Government of Rajasthan, Rajasthan State Universities, or Rajasthan State Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs).

Clause D

Central Govt / Defense Posted

Children of Central Government, Defense, or Central PSU employees currently stationed in Rajasthan for at least 1 continuous year prior to the REAP application deadline.

Seat Conversion & De-Reservation Protocol

When reserved category seats remain vacant due to a lack of eligible candidates during counselling rounds, CEG Rajasthan executes systematic de-reservation in strict accordance with state statutes.

1

Horizontal PwD & Ex-S

Vacant PwD and Defense seats revert to their respective parent vertical category (e.g., OBC-PwD reverts to OBC General).

2

Female Horizontal Seats

Unfilled 25% female quota seats revert to male candidates of the exact same category (e.g., SC-Female converts to SC-Male).

3

TSP & MBC Conversion

Vacant ST-TSP seats convert to general ST seats. Vacant MBC seats convert to OBC-NCL category seats before spot round.

4

Final Spot Round

In the institutional spot/mop-up round, any unfilled SC, ST, OBC, MBC, or EWS seats convert to General Open merit seats.
