Golden Rule of REAP Choice Allocation

The REAP seat allocation software evaluates candidate preferences sequentially from Choice #1 down to your last choice. The moment an eligible vacant seat matches your merit rank and category quota, that seat is provisionally allotted, and all lower preferences (Choice #X+1 onwards) are discarded for that round.

Advice: Always list colleges and branches in the strict order of your genuine desire. Never place a lower-ranked or less-preferred college above your dream choice merely because you think your rank is safe for it!

The Complete REAP 2027 Counselling Lifecycle

From registration and choice locking to seat allotment, upward movement, and physical institute reporting — follow the comprehensive 10-step roadmap.

1

Online Registration & ₹885 Fee

Phase 1: Initiation

Candidate registers on the CEG Rajasthan portal by providing basic personal details, Aadhaar number, valid mobile number, and paying the non-refundable registration & counselling fee of ₹885/- via UPI, Net Banking, or e-Mitra.

  • Generates unique REAP 2027 Form Number
  • Non-refundable & non-transferable token
  • Compulsory pre-requisite for choice filling
2

Document Upload & Priority Selection

Phase 1: Verification

Candidates upload scanned copies of Class 10th & 12th marksheets, JEE Main 2027 scorecard (if applicable), Domicile certificate, Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/MBC/EWS), and Income certificate for TFWS consideration.

  • AICTE subject verification (PCM + optional)
  • Allocation under Priority 1, 2, or 3
  • Category verification (OBC-NCL within valid FY)
3

Publication of Merit List (CRL & Category)

Phase 2: Merit Hierarchy

CEG Rajasthan releases the official REAP 2027 Combined Merit List, followed by separate Priority Merit Lists (Priority 1: JEE Main CRL, Priority 2: 12th Board PCM %, Priority 3: Diploma %) and category-wise ranks.

  • Grievance redressal window (2–3 days)
  • Final merit ranking determines allotment turns
  • Separate merit lists for TFWS & Kashmiri Migrants
4

Online Choice Filling & Preference Locking

Phase 2: Strategy

The heart of the counselling process. Candidates log in to the portal and select their desired combinations of colleges and engineering branches in strict descending order of preference. Unlimited choices can be added, reordered, or deleted.

  • No limit on total number of choices
  • Can choose GAS (Govt Aided) or SFS (Self-Financed)
  • Automatic locking at 5:00 PM on the final day
5

Round 1 Allotment (Outside Rajasthan & Special Quotas)

Phase 3: Initial Allotment

CEG releases seat allotments for 15% Outside Rajasthan (OR) candidates, as well as special categories (Ex-Servicemen, PwD, Kashmiri Migrants, and TFWS seats). Candidates download their Provisional Seat Allotment Letter.

  • Allotted solely on JEE Main 2027 CRL rank
  • Allotment letter mentions reporting college & dates
  • Option to apply for Upward Movement 1
6

Upward Movement 1 (Round 1 Upgradation)

Phase 3: Upgradation

Candidates allotted a seat in Round 1 who have completed provisional reporting can opt for Upward Movement on the portal. The system checks if higher-preference choices have become vacant due to non-reporting or higher cancellations.

  • Old seat is retained if no higher seat is vacant
  • Automatic upgrade if higher choice opens up
  • Zero fee penalty for participating
7

Round 2 Allotment (Main Rajasthan State Domicile 85%)

Phase 4: Main State Allotment

The largest round of REAP counselling. Allotted seats across all government and private engineering institutions in Rajasthan for Rajasthan Domicile candidates across General, OBC-NCL, SC, ST, MBC, and EWS categories under Priority 1, 2, and 3.

  • Fills all 85% domicile quota seats
  • Strict adherence to state reservation percentage
  • Results declared with college & branch allotment letter
8

Upward Movement 2 (Crucial Round 2 Upgradation)

Phase 4: State Upgradation

Rajasthan domicile candidates who reported physically or confirmed provisional acceptance can opt for Upward Movement 2. This is the last centralized upgradation round before seats are transferred to individual colleges for spot rounds.

  • Highest number of branch upgrades occur here
  • Existing seat remains 100% safe if upgrade fails
  • If upgraded, old seat is allocated to next deserving rank
9

Physical Institute Reporting & Document Verification

Phase 5: Final Admission

Candidate must visit the finally allotted engineering college in person with original credentials, 2 sets of attested photocopies, medical certificate, and pay the requisite college tuition fee token to confirm final admission.

  • Original document verification by college scrutiny team
  • Fee adjustment of previously paid acceptance fees
  • Issue of official College Roll Number & Admission Slip
10

Internal Sliding & Special Spot Rounds

Phase 5: Spot Admissions

(A) Internal Sliding: Conducted inside individual colleges among reported students to upgrade branches. (B) Spot Round: Direct physical open-house counselling conducted at college campuses to fill all remaining dropout / vacant seats.

  • Direct spot application for unregistered candidates too
  • On-the-spot merit calculation and same-day admission
  • Final culmination of REAP 2027 admission cycle

REAP Upward Movement — Zero-Risk Seat Upgradation

Understand the mathematical guarantee of REAP's Upward Movement. Try our interactive visual simulator to see how your choices are evaluated without risking your confirmed seat.

How the Upward Movement Algorithm Protects You

Suppose you submitted 25 college-branch choices and in Round 2, you were allotted Choice #12: MBM Jodhpur Electrical Engineering.

Choices #1 to #11 (Active) The software evaluates strictly Choices #1 to #11. If a seat in any of these 11 becomes vacant due to higher-rank movement, you are automatically upgraded!
Choice #12 (100% Reserved) If none of Choices #1 to #11 have an opening, your Choice #12 remains 100% yours. You CANNOT lose your existing seat!
Choices #13 to #25 (Eliminated) The algorithm will NEVER demote you to Choice #13 or below. Downward movement is mathematically impossible in REAP.

REAP 2027 Rounds Matrix & Quota Breakdown

Understand who participates in which round, how seats shift across categories, and how spot rounds are conducted for leftover engineering vacancies.

Round 1 Allotment

Outside Rajasthan (OR) & Special Categories

Solely dedicated to 15% Outside Rajasthan candidates across India, as well as supernumerary categories including TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver), Kashmiri Migrants (KM), and PwD candidates.

  • Seat Pool: 15% All India Quota + 5% TFWS
  • Merit Basis: JEE Main CRL Rank exclusively
  • Reporting: Online acceptance or designated nodal college
  • Upward Eligible: Yes, via Upward Movement 1
Upward Movement 1

Round 1 Upgradation Window

Online upgradation round for Round 1 allotted candidates who reported or confirmed acceptance. Seats vacated by students opting for JoSAA / CSAB are reallocated to higher preferences.

  • Eligibility: Candidates allotted in Round 1
  • Mechanism: Searches strictly Choices #1 to #X-1
  • Risk: 0% risk; existing seat remains secure
  • Result: Upgraded allotment letter issued
Round 2 Allotment

Main Rajasthan State Domicile (85%)

The central, largest counselling round in Rajasthan. Allotted to bonafide residents of Rajasthan across General, OBC-NCL, SC, ST, MBC, and EWS categories under Priority 1, 2, and 3.

  • Seat Pool: 85% Rajasthan Domicile Quota
  • Merit Hierarchy: Priority 1 > Priority 2 > Priority 3
  • Colleges: All Govt Autonomous, GECs & Private inst.
  • Reporting: Physical institute verification or Upward 2 opt-in
Upward Movement 2

State Domicile Upgradation Window

The most crucial round for Rajasthan state students. Candidates who reported in Round 2 can upgrade their branch (e.g. Mechanical to Electrical or ECE to CSE) as non-reported seats open up.

  • Eligibility: Round 2 confirmed candidates
  • Impact: Thousands of branch upgrades occur here
  • Safety: Retain previous college if no upgrade occurs
  • Action: Physical reporting at newly upgraded institute
Internal Sliding

College-Level Branch Reshuffling

Conducted physically inside the campus of each engineering college among candidates who have completed reporting. Allows upgrading to a better branch within the SAME college.

  • Venue: Campus auditorium / seminar hall
  • Scope: Within the same allotted college only
  • Eligibility: Only physically admitted students
  • Speed: Instant merit sliding and token transfer
Special Spot Round

Open House Institute Counselling

Direct physical spot round conducted on college campuses for all unfilled, dropped, or cancelled seats. Open to REAP registered, unallotted, and fresh walk-in applicants.

  • Seat Matrix: Published college-wise on DTE portal
  • Fresh Candidates: Permitted with ₹885 spot fee
  • Decision: Same-day document check & spot admission
  • Concludes: Officially wraps up REAP 2027 admissions

Strategic Choice Filling Guide for REAP 2027

How to arrange your college and branch preferences to maximize your admission chances without regretting your allotment.

1. Strict Descending Preference

Always list branches in decreasing order of your personal desire. Never put a lower-ranked college above a top college simply because you believe your rank is sufficient. The algorithm stops at the first match!

2. GAS vs SFS Seat Ordering

Government colleges offer both GAS (Govt Aided - ₹15k–30k/yr) and SFS (Self Financed - ₹60k–85k/yr) seats. Place GAS seats above SFS seats for the same branch to save tuition fees.

3. Fill Maximum Choices

REAP has zero limit on choices. Add at least 40 to 80 choices spanning dream colleges, realistic targets, and safe fallback institutions. Leaving choices blank risks total non-allotment.

4. TFWS Choices Placement

If eligible for Tuition Fee Waiver (annual family income < ₹8 Lakhs), add TFWS seat codes for top branches at the very top of your preference list before regular GAS and SFS seats.

5. Saving vs Locking Choices

Keep saving your choices regularly. If you do not manually lock your choices before the deadline, the REAP portal automatically locks your last saved preferences at the deadline cutoff time.

6. Institute Tier Hierarchy

Standard proven preference hierarchy in Rajasthan: MBM Jodhpur > CTAE Udaipur > RTU Kota (UD) > GEC Bikaner > GEC Ajmer > GWECA Ajmer (Women) > GEC Bharatpur.

Candidates applying for TFWS must submit an official Income Certificate in the prescribed REAP proforma issued and verified by an Executive Magistrate or Tehsildar, proving that the total annual gross income of both parents from all sources is less than ₹8.00 Lakhs during the preceding financial year.
