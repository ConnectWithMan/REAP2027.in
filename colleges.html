Rajasthan Technical Universities & Affiliation System

Technical education under REAP is organized under statutory state universities. Understanding which university governs your chosen college helps in assessing exam patterns, curriculum revision, and degree recognition.

Primary State Tech University

Rajasthan Technical University (RTU), Kota

Established 2006 | Rawatbhata Road, Kota

Governs the majority of engineering colleges in Southern and Eastern Rajasthan, including Jaipur, Kota, Udaipur, Ajmer, Alwar, Bhilwara, and Jhalawar. Houses its own flagship University Departments offering Aeronautical and Petrochemical specializations.

Affiliates SKIT, JECRC, PCE, MLVTI, ECA
Western Rajasthan Tech University

Bikaner Technical University (BTU), Bikaner

Established 2017 | Karni Industrial Area, Bikaner

Formed to decentralize and enhance technical education across Western and Northern Rajasthan (Bikaner, Jodhpur, Sikar, Jhunjhunu, Churu, Sri Ganganagar, and Bharatpur). Governs ECB Bikaner, GEC Bharatpur, GWECA Ajmer, and JIET.

Affiliates ECB, GWECA, JIET, GEC Bharatpur
Autonomous State University

MBM University, Jodhpur

Established 1951 | Upgraded to University in 2021

Formerly Mugneeram Bangur Memorial Engineering College, now a full-fledged autonomous state university. It designs its own modern curricula, conducts separate examinations, and awards its own recognized engineering degree.

Ranked #1 State Engineering University
Agricultural & Tech Constituent

CTAE (MPUAT), Udaipur

Established 1964 | Constituent College of MPUAT

Constituent College of Technology & Engineering under Maharana Pratap University of Agriculture and Technology. Nationally acclaimed for Mining Engineering, Agricultural Engineering, and renewable energy research.

India's Leading Mining & Agri Tech Hub

Understanding REAP Seat Matrix & Fee Structures

Rajasthan Engineering Admission Process bifurcates seats into three distinct categories across colleges: GAS (Govt. Aided), SFS (Self-Financed), and TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver). Understanding this distinction is crucial to prevent unexpectedly high semester fee bills.

Pro Choice-Filling Strategy: Never put SFS branch choices ahead of GAS branch choices for the same institute! For instance, if you want Computer Science at MBM University Jodhpur, place MBM Jodhpur - CSE (GAS) as Preference #1, and MBM Jodhpur - CSE (SFS) as Preference #2. Placing SFS above GAS locks you into the higher fee bracket even if your merit rank was sufficient for a subsidized GAS seat.

Physical Reporting & Document Verification Guide

After seat allotment in REAP 2027, candidates must report to their allotted college with original certificates and fee payment. Here is the official checklist required at the verification counter.

REAP & Exam Credentials

  • Provisional REAP 2027 Seat Allotment Letter (downloaded from portal)
  • Duly signed REAP 2027 Application Form printout
  • REAP ₹885 Registration Fee Transaction Receipt
  • NTA JEE Main 2027 Official Scorecard & Admit Card

Academic Records

  • Class 10th Board Certificate & Marksheet (proof of date of birth)
  • Class 12th Board Science Marksheet & Passing Certificate
  • Diploma in Engineering Marksheets (for Lateral / Priority 3)
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) & Migration Certificate (original)

Domicile & Reservation Proofs

  • Rajasthan Domicile Certificate (Bonafide Resident proof)
  • Category Certificate (SC, ST, OBC-NCL, MBC-NCL, or EWS)
  • Valid Income Certificate (mandatory if admitted under TFWS)
  • Medical Fitness Certificate from a registered MBBS Medical Officer
Reporting Verification Advice: Carry at least 4 self-attested photocopies of each document along with 6 recent passport-size photographs identical to the one uploaded during REAP registration. Admission fees can be deposited via Demand Draft (in favor of the allotted institute) or online through the college portal counter.

Frequently Asked Questions: REAP Colleges

Clear answers to vital queries concerning college selection, CEG codes, seat reservation, and reporting protocols.

MBM University, Jodhpur (formerly MBM Engineering College, established in 1951) is universally recognized as the flagship government technical institution in Rajasthan under REAP. Following MBM, CTAE Udaipur (College of Technology and Engineering under MPUAT) and University Departments, RTU Kota represent the premier tier of state-funded engineering campuses.

GAS (Government Aided Seats) are heavily subsidized by the Department of Technical Education, Government of Rajasthan. Students only pay approx. ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per semester. GAS seats are exclusive to autonomous universities like MBM, CTAE, and RTU.

SFS (Self-Financed Seats) are non-subsidized, costing between ₹60,000 and ₹95,000 per semester. The curriculum, degree, professors, and laboratory infrastructure for both GAS and SFS students in the same branch are identical.

Outside Rajasthan candidates are allotted seats under a statutory 15% quota based exclusively on their NTA JEE Main 2027 All India CRL Rank. Round 1 of REAP seat allotment is dedicated entirely to Outside Rajasthan applicants. Both government autonomous colleges and RTU-affiliated private institutions are open for Outside Rajasthan candidates.

Each participating college is assigned a 3-digit institutional identifier by the Centre for Electronic Governance (CEG) Rajasthan (e.g., Code 001 for MBM University, 002 for CTAE Udaipur, 003 for RTU Kota, 023 for SKIT Jaipur). During online choice filling on reap.rajasthan.gov.in, filtering choices using the exact 3-digit code prevents accidental selection of similar-sounding institutions.

TFWS is an AICTE-mandated scheme providing 5% supernumerary seats in every branch of all approved colleges. Candidates selected under TFWS receive 100% waiver on tuition fees throughout the four-year course. To qualify, candidates must be Rajasthan Domiciles with total annual family income from all sources under ₹8.0 Lakh.

The premier private institutions affiliated with Rajasthan Technical University (RTU) in Jaipur include:

  • Swami Keshvanand Institute of Technology (SKIT), Jagatpura (NAAC A++)
  • Jaipur Engineering College and Research Centre (JECRC Foundation), Sitapura
  • Poornima College of Engineering (PCE), Sitapura
  • Arya College of Engineering & IT (ACEIT Main Campus), Kukas
  • Anand International College of Engineering (Anand-ICE), Agra Road
