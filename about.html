Official Background

What is REAP (Rajasthan Engineering Admission Process)?

The Rajasthan Engineering Admission Process (REAP) is the centralized, state-level online admission and counselling mechanism in Rajasthan. It governs admissions into 4-year undergraduate engineering programs (B.Tech / B.E.) and 5-year Architecture programs (B.Arch) across government universities, autonomous colleges, and private engineering institutions.

Conducted under the direct supervision of the Department of Technical Education, Government of Rajasthan, the administrative operations are executed annually by the Centre for Electronic Governance (CEG), Jaipur.

Seats are allocated strictly in order of academic merit across three distinct applicant categories: Priority 1 (JEE Main CRL Rank), Priority 2 (Class 12th Science Percentile), and Priority 3 (3-Year Engineering Diploma aggregate).
