The 3-Tier REAP Merit Priority System

How CEG Rajasthan ranks candidates and allocates engineering seats. Candidates in higher priority tiers are allotted seats first, regardless of the board percentage of lower-tier applicants.

Priority 1 (Highest)

JEE Main 2027 All India Rank (AIR / CRL)

Candidates possessing a valid All India Common Rank List (CRL) rank in JEE Main 2027 Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech) conducted by National Testing Agency (NTA).

  • 100% of seats in Round 1 (OR quota) allotted via P1
  • Round 2 allocates Rajasthan seats first to P1 candidates
  • Gets top college branches (MBM, CTAE, RTU CSE/IT)
  • Tie-breaking: NTA Math > Physics > Chemistry > Age
Priority 2 (Intermediate)

Class 12th Board Science (PCM) Percentile

Candidates who have not appeared for JEE Main 2027 or did not qualify, evaluated strictly on their Class 12th Board Science (PCM) percentile.

  • Allotted after exhausting Priority 1 applicants
  • Uses board-normalized percentile (RBSE / CBSE / ICSE)
  • High board percentile (90%+) secures Govt. college core seats
  • Tie-breaking: Math marks > Physics > Chem > Age
Priority 3 (Diploma)

Diploma in Engineering & Technology Percentage

Candidates who have completed a 3-year Diploma in Engineering from BTER or AICTE-approved polytechnic institutions applying for 1st-year engineering seats.

  • Allotted seats after Priority 1 and Priority 2 pools
  • Merit determined by final aggregate diploma percentage
  • Direct admission to 1st year (B.Tech)
  • For 2nd-year lateral entry, candidates apply via LEEP

Official REAP 2027 Tie-Breaking Protocol

When two or more candidates obtain identical scores, CEG resolves the tie using the following sequential hierarchy:

In Case of JEE Main Score Tie:
  1. Higher NTA score in Mathematics
  2. Higher NTA score in Physics
  3. Higher NTA score in Chemistry
  4. Candidate older in age (earlier Date of Birth)
In Case of 12th Board Percentile Tie:
  1. Higher absolute marks in Mathematics
  2. Higher absolute marks in Physics
  3. Higher absolute marks in Chemistry / Optional
  4. Candidate older in age (earlier Date of Birth)

Rajasthan Domicile (85%) vs. Outside Rajasthan (15%)

Understand the exact domicile criteria defining state quota eligibility, reservation benefits, and certificate issuing rules in Rajasthan.

Rajasthan State Quota (85%)

Home State

85% of sanctioned seats across all Government Engineering Colleges (GECs), University Departments (RTU Kota, MBM Jodhpur, CTAE Udaipur), and Private Colleges are reserved for bonafide residents of Rajasthan.

Eligible Domicile Categories:
  • Category A: Candidate is a continuous resident of Rajasthan for at least 10 years, or born in Rajasthan with parent domicile.
  • Category B: Children of serving / retired employees of Government of Rajasthan, State PSUs, Boards, or Universities.
  • Category C: Children of Central Govt. / Defence / Paramilitary personnel posted in Rajasthan or originating from Rajasthan.
Eligible for all caste reservations (SC/ST/OBC/MBC/EWS) and TFWS fee waiver.

Outside Rajasthan Quota (15%)

All India Seats

15% of seats in all engineering institutions are earmarked for candidates who do not possess Rajasthan state domicile certificate. These candidates participate under the Outside Rajasthan (OR) category.

Key Operational Conditions:
  • Admissions exclusively via Round 1: Allocated solely on the basis of JEE Main 2027 All India Rank (CRL).
  • Treated as General / Unreserved: No caste, tribe, or EWS concessions from home states are applicable in Rajasthan.
  • Ineligible for TFWS: Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme is strictly reserved for Rajasthan domicile applicants.
  • Equal Tuition Fee: College tuition fees are identical for Rajasthan and Outside Rajasthan students.
Outside Rajasthan candidates who miss Round 1 can participate directly in college-level Spot Rounds.

Crucial Warning on Creamy Layer Certificates

Candidates belonging to OBC or MBC categories whose parental income exceeds ₹8,00,000 are classified under the Creamy Layer. Creamy layer candidates are NOT eligible for reservation and must apply strictly under the General / Open category. Submitting an expired or invalid OBC-NCL certificate will result in immediate conversion to General category during document verification.

Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS) Explained

Save ₹70,000 to ₹1,20,000 per year throughout all 4 years of engineering. Learn who qualifies for 5% supernumerary TFWS seats and how to apply.

TFWS Mandatory Eligibility Rules

  • State Domicile: Strictly reserved for Rajasthan state domicile candidates.
  • Parental Annual Income: Total family income from all sources must be less than ₹8,00,000 (8 Lakhs) per annum.
  • Merit Allotment: Allotted strictly on merit during Round 1 of counselling.
  • Separate Branch Codes: TFWS seats have distinct college choice codes ending in (TFWS). Candidates must opt for TFWS in choice filling.
  • Supernumerary Nature: 5% seats over and above the sanctioned branch intake (e.g., 6 extra seats in a 120-seat branch).

Financial Concession & Fee Details

Students admitted under TFWS pay zero tuition fee. Only non-tuition institute charges apply:

Age Limit & Medical Fitness Standards

CEG Rajasthan and AICTE regulations on age eligibility and physical fitness requirements demanded during institutional document verification.

Age Limit Regulations

In accordance with current AICTE norms and Technical Education Department notifications:

  • Upper Age Limit: There is NO upper age barrier for participating in REAP 2027 counselling or seeking admission to B.Tech, B.E., or B.Arch courses.
  • Minimum Age: There is no specified minimum age limit; however, the candidate must have passed the qualifying 10+2 / Diploma examination.
  • Date of Birth Verification: Candidate's Date of Birth will be verified from the Class 10th (Secondary) Board Certificate for tie-breaking procedures.

Physical Fitness Standards

Every candidate must submit a Medical Fitness Certificate issued by a Registered Medical Practitioner (Govt. Medical Officer or MBBS) during physical reporting:

  • Minimum Height: Male candidates: 1.5 meters; Female candidates: 1.4 meters.
  • Chest Expansion: Minimum 5 cm expansion for male candidates.
  • Vision: Better eye 6/6, worse eye 6/9 with or without glasses.
  • Colour Blindness: Colour blind candidates are NOT eligible for Mining Engineering and Petroleum Engineering disciplines.
  • Hearing: Normal hearing capacity (hearing aids permitted).
