REAP 2027 Merit List & Official Cutoff Benchmarks

Understanding the 3-tier admission priority hierarchy, JEE Main percentile-to-rank conversions, and expected cutoffs for premier Rajasthan engineering colleges.

REAP 2027 Admission & Merit Priority Order:
Priority 1: JEE Main 2027 Rank > Priority 2: 12th Marks

REAP does not conduct an independent entrance examination. Seats are allotted strictly based on merit: First preference is given to candidates with valid JEE Main 2027 ranks. If seats remain vacant, candidates are allotted based on Class 12th Science (PCM) percentile, followed by Diploma in Engineering percentage. Separate category-wise merit lists (General, SC, ST, OBC, MBC, EWS) are released by CEG Rajasthan.

REAP 2027 — Counselling Rounds & Upward Movement Protocol

CEG Rajasthan conducts a streamlined centralized online seat allotment process. Understand each stage, reporting rules, and seat upgradation mechanisms.

Round 1

OR, TFWS & Special Quota

Allotment for Outside Rajasthan (OR - 15% quota), Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS), Ex-Servicemen, PwD, and Kashmiri Migrants (KM) based on merit.

Reporting Mandatory: Download Provisional Allotment Letter. Report physically with original documents + 2 photocopy sets and pay college fees.
Round 2

Rajasthan State Domicile Round

Main allotment for Rajasthan domicile candidates across General, OBC-NCL, SC, ST, MBC, and EWS categories as per state merit rank and locked choices.

Allotment Action: Freeze seat by reporting to college and depositing fee, OR report & deposit fee to become eligible for Upward Movement.
Upward Movement

Online Upgradation Round

Permits admitted candidates to compete for higher preferences. Crucial Rule: Candidate MUST have reported to the allotted college and paid the fee to apply.

Upward Rules: If upgraded to a higher choice, previous seat is automatically cancelled. If not upgraded, original seat is retained safely.
Internal Sliding & Spot

Institutional Sliding / Spot Round

College-level branch sliding for admitted candidates within the same institute, followed by Spot Round (Direct Admission) for any remaining vacant seats.

Spot Guidelines: Open for all eligible registered/unregistered candidates as per AICTE and CEG guidelines directly at college campus.

REAP 2027 Choice Filling & Locking Protocol

Choice filling is the most crucial determinant of your college allotment. Follow these official guidelines from CEG Rajasthan:

  • Fill Maximum Options: There is no limit on the number of choices. Enter dream colleges first, practical choices second, and safe colleges at the bottom to avoid non-allotment.
  • Frequent Saving: Save your preference list frequently during your session to avoid timeout or accidental data loss.
  • Choice Locking: Lock your options only when completely satisfied. You will be prompted for password or OTP authentication to confirm locking.
  • System Auto-Lock: If choices are saved but not manually locked by the deadline, the REAP server will automatically lock saved choices. However, never leave it to the last moment.
  • Unlock Window: If CEG announces a grievance/unlock window, candidates can submit an unlock request to rearrange choices before the allotment run.

REAP 2027 — Rajasthan Reservation Matrix & TFWS Scheme

Official state quota distribution, vertical category reservation percentages, horizontal sub-quotas, and 100% Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS).

1. Vertical Category Quotas (State)

Applicable to 85% Rajasthan state domicile seats:

  • General / Unreserved: 36% (open state merit)
  • OBC-NCL: 21% (Non-Creamy Layer certificate required)
  • SC (Scheduled Caste): 16% reservation
  • ST (Scheduled Tribe): 12% reservation (with 45% TSP sub-quota in scheduled tribal areas)
  • MBC (Most Backward Classes): 5% reservation
  • EWS (Economically Weaker Section): 10% reservation (family income < ₹8 LPA)

2. Horizontal & Special Quotas

Applied horizontally across each category:

  • Women / Girls Candidates: 25% horizontal reservation across all categories.
  • PwD Candidates: 5% horizontal reservation within each category (min 40% disability certified by medical board).
  • Ex-Servicemen / Defence (Ex-S): Priority-based sub-quota for wards/widows of armed forces.
  • Kashmiri Migrants (KM): 5% supernumerary seats for displaced Kashmiri Pandits / Migrants.
  • Outside Rajasthan (OR): 15% of total seats reserved for non-domicile candidates.

3. TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme)

AICTE-approved fee waiver scheme:

  • 5% Supernumerary Seats: Available in all AICTE-approved engineering institutions across Rajasthan.
  • Eligibility Income: Total annual family income from all sources must be less than ₹8,00,000.
  • 100% Tuition Fee Exemption: Students pay zero tuition fee throughout 4 years of B.Tech. Only development & exam fees apply.
  • Separate TFWS Merit: Allotted strictly in Round 1 based on JEE Main / Class 12th merit among applicants who uploaded valid income certificates.

6-Stage REAP 2027 Admission Roadmap

Step-by-step walkthrough from initial eligibility qualification to final enrollment at your allotted engineering institution.

01
Qualify JEE Main or 12th Board / DiplomaStage 1

Obtain your JEE Main 2027 score card (for Priority 1 admissions) or secure 45%+ (40% for reserved) in Class 12th Science PCM / 3-year Diploma. Candidates aiming for B.Arch must secure valid NATA 2027 or JEE Paper 2 scores.

02
Online Registration & ₹885 Fee PaymentStage 2

Register on the official REAP 2027 portal (ceg.rajasthan.gov.in / reap2027.in). Pay the non-refundable registration fee of ₹885/- online. Fill academic scores, personal data, and upload scanned photographs, signature, and category/income certificates.

03
Merit List Verification & Grievance ResolutionStage 3

CEG Rajasthan publishes provisional merit lists for JEE Main, 12th Board, and Diploma streams. Verify your merit rank and category details. Submit objections during the grievance window if any discrepancy exists before the final merit list is released.

04
Online Choice Filling & LockingStage 4

Log in to the candidate panel to enter college and branch choices in decreasing order of true preference. Fill maximum available choices. Save frequently and lock your choices with OTP/password before the deadline to ensure your options are considered.

05
Seat Allotment Result & Provisional LetterStage 5

Check seat allotment status on the REAP website. If allotted a seat, download the official Provisional Seat Allotment Letter. It contains college and branch information, reporting venue, required documents, and fee payment instructions.

06
Physical College Reporting & Upward MovementStage 6

Mandatory Step: Report in-person to the allotted institute with original documents, 2 sets of self-attested photocopies, medical certificate, and pay admission fees. Only after reporting can you opt for online Upward Movement for a higher preference.
