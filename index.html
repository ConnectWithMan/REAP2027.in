Overview & Purpose

What is REAP 2027?

The Rajasthan Engineering Admission Process (REAP) is the centralized counselling mechanism for undergraduate engineering (B.Tech / B.E.) admissions across Rajasthan government and private colleges.

🏛️

Centralised State Admissions

Single-window admission portal managed by the Centre for Electronic Governance (CEG) and Rajasthan Technical University (RTU) Kota for streamlined college placement.

🎯

Dual Merit Criteria

Seats are allotted first on the basis of national JEE Main (Paper 1) ranks, followed by Class 12 (10+2) PCM percentage for remaining open seats.

💻

100% Online Procedure

From initial registration, fee payment, and digital document upload to choice locking and seat allotment letters, the entire preliminary process is handled online.

📜

RTU & BTU Affiliation

Covers technical degree programs affiliated with Rajasthan Technical University (RTU, Kota) and Bikaner Technical University (BTU, Bikaner).

⚖️

State Domicile Reservation

Statutory reservation benefits for Rajasthan domicile applicants under SC, ST, OBC-NCL, MBC-NCL, EWS, and special horizontal categories (PwD, Defence, KM).

🔄

Multi-Round Upgradation

Features 3 to 4 sequential counselling rounds, upward movement opportunities, and physical institute-level spot rounds to ensure zero seat wastage.

Qualifying Criteria

REAP 2027 Eligibility Criteria & Reservations

Ensure you satisfy all academic, age, domicile, and category preconditions before applying for REAP 2027 counselling.

Academic Qualifications

  • Compulsory Subjects: Passed 10+2 (Senior Secondary) examination with Physics and Mathematics as mandatory subjects.
  • Optional Subject: Any one from Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, Computer Science, Information Technology, or Technical Vocational subjects.
  • General / Unreserved Minimum: Minimum 45% aggregate marks in the qualifying 3-subject combination.
  • Reserved Categories Minimum: Minimum 40% aggregate marks for SC, ST, OBC-NCL, MBC-NCL, and PwD candidates of Rajasthan domicile.
  • Diploma Holders: Candidates holding an AICTE-approved 3-year Diploma in Engineering with at least 45% marks (40% for reserved) are also eligible.

Admission Priorities & Domicile

  • Priority 1 — JEE Main: Candidates with a valid JEE Main 2027 Paper-1 (B.E./B.Tech) CRL score receive first priority in seat allocation.
  • Priority 2 — Class 12 Marks: Candidates who have not appeared for JEE Main are considered based on Class 12 PCM percentage.
  • Rajasthan Domicile: Required for availing state category reservations (SC/ST/OBC/MBC/EWS/TFWS) and 85% state quota seats.
  • Out-of-Rajasthan Candidates: Eligible under the 15% All-India Quota seats based on JEE Main scores.
  • Age Limit: No upper age restriction as per AICTE and RTU admission guidelines.

Document Checklist

Documents Required for REAP 2027

Keep both digital scanned copies (PDF/JPEG) for online registration and physical original documents with at least 4 photocopied sets for college reporting.

1. Academic & Exam Proofs

  • Class 10 (Secondary) mark sheet and passing certificate (Date of Birth proof).
  • Class 12 (Senior Secondary) original mark sheet and certificate.
  • JEE Main 2027 Score Card and Admit Card (for rank holders).
  • School / College Leaving Certificate (CLC) or Transfer Certificate (TC).
  • Migration Certificate issued by the 10+2 education board.
  • Character Certificate from the principal of the last attended institution.

2. Identity & State Certificates

  • Valid Government Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card / Passport / Voter ID).
  • Rajasthan Bonafide Domicile Resident Certificate.
  • Caste Certificate (SC / ST / OBC-NCL / MBC-NCL) issued by competent authority.
  • Income Certificate / EWS Certificate (required for TFWS and EWS quota).
  • Disability Certificate issued by a recognized Medical Board (for PwD).
  • 6 to 8 passport-size color photographs matching the online registration upload.
📄
Reporting Requirement: After seat allotment, candidates must present all original documents along with the printed REAP Provisional Allotment Letter and online fee receipt at the reporting institution for in-person document validation.

REAP Counselling Application Fee

A non-refundable registration fee of approximately ₹500 to ₹1,000 (category-dependent) is payable online via Net Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card, or UPI during the registration phase.

Seat Acceptance Advance

Upon provisional allotment in Round 1 or 2, candidates must pay a seat commitment / acceptance fee (typically ₹10,000 – ₹20,000) online, which gets adjusted against college tuition upon reporting.

Admission Roadmap

Step-by-Step REAP 2027 Counselling Process

Follow these 6 sequential steps to secure your engineering seat through the official REAP 2027 counselling portal.

  1. 1. Online Registration & Fee Payment Access the official REAP 2027 portal, enter candidate personal details, qualifying exam roll numbers (JEE Main 2027 / Class 12), and pay the non-refundable registration fee.
  2. 2. Uploading Scanned Credentials Upload high-resolution scans of your photographs, signatures, mark sheets, Rajasthan domicile certificate, and caste / category certificates as per specified dimensions.
  3. 3. Publication of Provisional Merit List CEG Rajasthan issues preliminary merit rankings categorized by JEE Main rank holders and Class 12 applicants. Verify all data and file objections if discrepancies exist.
  4. 4. Choice Filling & Preference Locking Browse eligible colleges and disciplines. Build your preferred list in descending priority order. Lock choices securely before the scheduled portal deadline.
  5. 5. Seat Allotment Result Declaration Download your Provisional Seat Allotment Letter generated based on your merit position, reservation category, and locked preference order.
  6. 6. Fee Remittance & College Physical Reporting Remit the seat acceptance fee online, download the payment confirmation, and report in-person to the allotted institution with original documents for final admission.

Essential Application Advice

  • Keep multiple color photocopies of every certificate ready before the reporting window starts.
  • Double-check spelling of candidate, father's, and mother's names against Class 10 certificates.
  • Ensure caste certificates for OBC/MBC-NCL are issued within the valid financial year.
  • Never skip the upward movement option if you desire a higher college or branch preference.
  • Attend physical spot counselling if you missed registration or seek last-minute upgrades.
💡
Pro Tip on Choice Locking: The REAP algorithm evaluates choices from Choice #1 downwards. Never list a less-desired college above a better one simply because of proximity.
Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions About REAP 2027

Find quick and authoritative answers to the most common queries regarding Rajasthan engineering admissions.

Is JEE Main compulsory to apply for REAP 2027?
No, JEE Main is not mandatory, but it is strongly recommended. REAP reserves first priority in seat allotment for candidates holding valid JEE Main (Paper 1) ranks. Candidates without JEE Main are considered based on their Class 12 PCM aggregate marks in subsequent allocations.
Can students from states outside Rajasthan apply for REAP 2027?
Yes. Candidates from outside Rajasthan can apply under the 15% All-India Quota for private engineering colleges and designated seats in government colleges based on JEE Main scores. However, Rajasthan domicile reservation benefits (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) are exclusive to state residents.
What is the minimum percentage required in Class 12 for REAP 2027?
Candidates must score at least 45% aggregate marks in Physics, Mathematics, and one optional subject (Chemistry / Computer Science / Biology / Biotechnology) for General / Unreserved category. Candidates belonging to SC / ST / OBC-NCL / MBC-NCL / PwD of Rajasthan must secure at least 40% aggregate marks.
What is the Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS) in REAP?
TFWS is an AICTE-mandated scheme offering 5% supernumerary seats in every participating institute. Under TFWS, students with a family annual income of less than ₹8.00 Lakhs pay zero tuition fees throughout the 4-year B.Tech program. Applicable only to Rajasthan domicile candidates.
How many counselling rounds are conducted in REAP?
REAP typically conducts 3 to 4 centralized online allotment rounds (covering Out-of-Rajasthan, Rajasthan Domicile, and TFWS categories), followed by Upward Movement for upgraded seats, and a concluding Institute-level Physical Spot Round for leftover vacancies.
What is the difference between GAS and SFS seats?
GAS (Government Aided Seats) have heavily subsidized tuition fees (approx. ₹35,000 – ₹60,000/yr) funded by the state government. SFS (Self-Financed Scheme) seats in the same government colleges carry higher fees (approx. ₹65,000 – ₹95,000/yr) to support infrastructure and faculty costs.
How does the Upward Movement round work in REAP?
If you are allotted a seat that wasn't your first choice, you can accept the seat, pay the seat acceptance fee, report to the college, and opt for "Upward Movement". If seats vacate in your higher-preference choices in subsequent rounds, you will be automatically upgraded without losing your original seat.
What happens if I fail to report to the allotted college on time?
Failing to report physically at the allotted institution within the prescribed dates will result in the immediate cancellation of your allotted seat. You may also forfeit your eligibility for standard upward movement rounds, leaving the spot round as your only remaining path.
What is the REAP 2027 application registration fee?
The non-refundable application registration fee is expected to be ₹700 to ₹1,000 for General category applicants, and ₹350 to ₹500 for reserved category applicants of Rajasthan domicile. Exact amounts will be notified in the official REAP 2027 information brochure.
Is there direct admission or management quota under REAP?
Yes, private engineering colleges in Rajasthan affiliated with RTU / BTU typically reserve 15% management quota seats filled directly at the institutional level, alongside direct spot admissions for vacant seats after centralized REAP counselling concludes.
