What is REAP 2027?

The Rajasthan Engineering Admission Process (REAP) is the centralised counselling conducted for admission to first-year B.Tech programmes at Rajasthan Technical University (RTU) and its affiliated government and private engineering colleges. Candidates who qualify through JEE Main, or meet the applicable state-level criteria, register on the REAP portal, fill college and branch preferences, and are allotted seats across multiple rounds based on rank, category and choice order. REAP 2027 will govern admissions for the 2027–28 academic session.

Eligibility

  • Passed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics from a recognised board
  • Minimum qualifying marks as prescribed by RTU for the applicable category
  • Valid JEE Main score, or state-level qualifying criteria where applicable
  • Domicile of Rajasthan required for certain reserved-seat categories

Seats & colleges

  • Government engineering colleges affiliated to RTU
  • Private engineering colleges participating in REAP counselling
  • Branches span Computer Science, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, and allied disciplines
  • Seat matrix published separately for each participating institute

Documents required

  • Class 10 and Class 12 mark sheets and certificates
  • JEE Main 2027 rank card / scorecard
  • Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) where applicable
  • Rajasthan domicile certificate, for domicile-based seats
  • Passport-size photograph and scanned signature
  • Valid photo ID (Aadhaar, school ID, or equivalent)

Fee structure

  • Application/registration fee — category-wise, announced with the official notification
  • Choice-locking fee at the time of filling preferences
  • Seat acceptance / confirmation fee after allotment
  • Reporting fee payable at the allotted institute

Exact amounts are fixed by RTU each year — refer to the official REAP notification before making any payment.

Participating colleges & reservation categories

College types

  • Government engineering colleges under RTU
  • Government-aided institutes
  • Self-financed private engineering colleges

Reservation categories

  • SC / ST / OBC / MBC / EWS as per Rajasthan government norms
  • Persons with Disability (PwD) horizontal reservation
  • Defence, freedom fighter and sportsperson quotas
  • TFW (Tuition Fee Waiver) seats for eligible candidates

Application & counselling process

  1. Register on the official REAP portal with basic academic and personal details.
  2. Upload required documents — mark sheets, category certificate, domicile proof.
  3. Pay the application fee and submit the form before the deadline.
  4. Fill college and branch choices in order of preference during choice filling.
  5. Check seat allotment results and report to the allotted college within the given window.
  6. Pay confirmation fee and complete document verification to secure the seat.

How the merit list works

  • Seats are allotted primarily on JEE Main All India Rank (AIR), category-wise
  • Within each category, candidates are ranked and matched to choices in order
  • Ties at the same rank are typically resolved using Class 12 marks, then date of birth, as per RTU's published tie-breaking rule
  • A candidate is allotted the highest-preference choice their rank makes possible in that round

Choice filling — what matters

  • List every college-branch combination you'd genuinely accept, not just top picks
  • Order choices by real preference, not just branch popularity — a lower-ranked choice you'd accept is better than leaving options out
  • Choices can usually be edited until the lock deadline for each round — review before locking
  • An unlocked or unfilled choice list can mean no allotment in that round

Scholarships & fee waivers

  • TFW (Tuition Fee Waiver) seats for candidates from economically weaker backgrounds meeting income criteria
  • Rajasthan state government scholarship schemes for SC/ST/OBC/minority students
  • National Means-cum-Merit and post-matric scholarships, applied for separately after admission
  • Some private colleges offer merit-based fee concessions — check with the institute directly

Important instructions

  • Use a working mobile number and email — all REAP communication goes through these
  • Keep scanned documents ready in the specified format and size before starting registration
  • Fees paid for registration and choice-locking are generally non-refundable
  • Once a seat is accepted and reported, exit or upgrade rules follow RTU's published policy for that round

Helpdesk & support

  • Official REAP helpdesk contact details are published with the counselling notification each year
  • For portal or login issues, use the technical support contact listed on the REAP website
  • For document or eligibility queries, colleges' individual admission cells can also assist
  • Always cross-check any phone number or email against the official REAP portal before sharing personal documents

Frequently asked questions

Is JEE Main compulsory for REAP 2027?

Most seats are filled using JEE Main rank; some categories may follow state-specific criteria. Confirm against the official notification once released.

Can candidates from outside Rajasthan apply?

Yes, general-category seats are open to candidates from other states, while certain reserved seats require Rajasthan domicile.

How many rounds of counselling are held?

REAP typically runs multiple allotment rounds followed by a spot round, depending on seat availability.

Where will the official schedule be published?

On the official REAP / RTU counselling portal. This page should be updated once that notification is out.

What documents are required for REAP 2027 registration?

Class 10 and 12 mark sheets, JEE Main scorecard, category certificate where applicable, domicile certificate, passport-size photograph and signature scan, and valid ID proof.

What is the application fee for REAP 2027?

The fee varies by category and is announced with the official notification; general category candidates typically pay a higher fee than reserved categories.

How is the REAP merit list decided?

Seats are allotted mainly on JEE Main All India Rank within each category. Ties are generally resolved using Class 12 marks and then date of birth, per RTU's published rule.

Are REAP registration and choice-locking fees refundable?

No, fees paid for registration and choice-locking are generally non-refundable under RTU's published policy.
