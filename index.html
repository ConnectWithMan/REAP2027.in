What is REAP 2027?

REAP is the centralised counselling process for admission to first-year B.Tech courses in Rajasthan Technical University-affiliated engineering colleges. It helps candidates register, choose preferred colleges and branches, and secure a seat based on rank, category, and availability.

Admission route

Centralised counselling for engineering admissions across Rajasthan.

Who can apply

Students seeking first-year B.Tech seats in RTU and participating institutes.

Selection basis

Rank, category, domicile rules, and preference order during choice filling.

Eligibility

  • Passed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics from a recognised board
  • Met the minimum qualifying marks for the applicable category
  • Have a valid JEE Main score or satisfy the relevant state criteria
  • Fulfill Rajasthan domicile conditions for reserved-seat categories

Colleges & seats

  • Government engineering colleges affiliated to RTU
  • Private engineering colleges participating in REAP counselling
  • Branches include CSE, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical and related fields
  • Each institute publishes a separate seat matrix for counselling

Documents required

  • Class 10 and Class 12 mark sheets and certificates
  • JEE Main scorecard or rank card
  • Category certificate if applicable
  • Rajasthan domicile certificate for domicile-based seats
  • Passport-size photograph and scanned signature
  • Valid identity proof

Fee structure

  • Registration fee, announced with the official notification
  • Choice-filling or preference-processing charges where applicable
  • Seat acceptance fee after allotment
  • Reporting fee at the allotted institute

Exact amounts are fixed by RTU each year and should be checked on the official counselling portal before payment.

Participating institutions

College types

  • Government engineering colleges under RTU
  • Government-aided institutions
  • Private engineering colleges participating in REAP

Reservation categories

  • SC, ST, OBC, MBC and EWS as per Rajasthan rules
  • PwD horizontal reservation
  • Defence, freedom fighter and sports quotas
  • TFW seats for eligible candidates

Application & counselling process

  1. Register on the official REAP portal and create your application profile.
  2. Upload required documents, including marksheets and category proofs if required.
  3. Pay the application fee and submit the form before the deadline.
  4. Fill preferred colleges and branches in order of preference.
  5. Check allotment results and complete reporting within the given window.
  6. Pay the acceptance fee and complete verification to secure the seat.

Frequently asked questions

Is JEE Main compulsory for REAP 2027?

Most seats are filled using JEE Main rank; some categories may follow state-specific criteria. Confirm against the official notification once released.

Can candidates from outside Rajasthan apply?

Yes, general-category seats are open to candidates from other states, while certain reserved seats require Rajasthan domicile.

How many rounds of counselling are held?

REAP typically runs multiple allotment rounds followed by a spot round, depending on seat availability.

Where will the official schedule be published?

On the official REAP / RTU counselling portal. This page should be updated once that notification is out.

What documents are required for REAP 2027 registration?

Class 10 and 12 mark sheets, JEE Main scorecard, category certificate where applicable, domicile certificate, passport-size photograph and signature scan, and valid ID proof.

What is the application fee for REAP 2027?

The fee varies by category and is announced with the official notification; general category candidates typically pay a higher fee than reserved categories.
