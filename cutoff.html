REAP 3-Tier Admission Merit Hierarchy

CEG Rajasthan executes seat allocation strictly according to a statutory 3-tier merit ladder. Understand why JEE Main CRL rank holders always take precedence over Board PCM applicants.

Priority 1

JEE Main 2027 All India CRL

Candidates applying with a valid NTA JEE Main 2027 All India Common Rank List (CRL) rank. They receive absolute first preference across all seats in Round 1 (Outside Rajasthan) and Round 2 (Rajasthan Domicile).

Golden Rule: Even a candidate with 12th Board PCM 99% cannot take a seat if an eligible JEE Main CRL rank holder has locked that choice.
Priority 2

Class 12th Board PCM %

Candidates applying through aggregate percentage/percentile marks in Physics, Mathematics, and one optional technical subject in 10+2 (RBSE, CBSE, ICSE, or State Boards).

Allotment Timing: Priority 2 candidates are allotted seats only after the entire list of Priority 1 candidates is completely exhausted.
Priority 3

3-Year Engineering Diploma

Candidates holding an AICTE/BTER approved 3-Year Engineering Diploma seeking first-year B.Tech admission based on final diploma percentage.

Vacant Intake: Considered after Priority 1 and Priority 2 for remaining vacancies prior to decentralized spot rounds.

Key Factors Influencing REAP 2027 Cutoff Fluctuations

Total Applicant Volume

Higher number of registered JEE Main candidates in Rajasthan drives closing ranks upward in high-demand CSE & AI specializations.

New AI/Data Science Seats

Approval of additional intake in AI & DS and Cyber Security expands overall tech seats, stabilizing pure CSE cutoffs.

JoSAA / CSAB Synchronization

When JoSAA round 5 and CSAB special rounds finalize, candidates exit REAP, creating deep cutoff drops during REAP Upward Movement.

Fee Subsidies (GAS vs SFS)

Lower annual tuition fees in GAS seats (~₹40,000/yr vs ~₹90,000/yr in SFS) cause GAS seats to close 20k–50k ranks earlier.

Government-Aided (GAS) vs Self-Financed (SFS) Cutoffs

Why does MBM Jodhpur CSE close at Rank 65,000 under GAS but Rank 95,000 under SFS? Unpack the exact fee, curriculum, and allotment differences.

GAS (Government-Aided Seat)

Government Aided Seats receive direct state grants, keeping semester tuition fees minimal. Because of the exceptional Return on Investment (ROI), GAS seats represent the highest competitive cutoff in REAP.

  • Semester Tuition Fee: ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 (Approx.)
  • Cutoff Benchmark: Top 5% to 8% of all REAP applicants
  • Availability: Only in Government Universities & Colleges (MBM, CTAE, RTU, etc.)
  • Seat Code: Designated with "-GAS" in REAP choice locking
Highest ROI & Lowest Student Debt

SFS (Self-Financed Seat)

SFS seats are operated on a self-supporting financial model approved by the state fee committee. Critically, students attend identical classrooms, take exams together, and receive the identical degree and placement opportunities as GAS students.

  • Semester Tuition Fee: ₹35,000 to ₹50,000 (Approx.)
  • Cutoff Benchmark: 25,000 to 50,000 ranks more relaxed than GAS
  • Availability: In both Government Colleges and All Private Institutes
  • Seat Code: Designated with "-SFS" in REAP choice locking

Round-Wise Cutoff Shift & Upward Movement Impact

How REAP cutoffs dynamically relax across admission rounds. Don't panic if your first-round allotment isn't your dream branch—Upward Movement opens premium seats.

Round 1: Outside Rajasthan (15%)

All India JEE Main CRL candidates only.

Cutoffs are steep due to limited 15% quota. CSE in MBM closes around 45,000–60,000 CRL.

Round 2: Rajasthan Domicile (85%)

Priority 1 & Priority 2 Candidates.

Major allotment round where state domicile reservations and sub-quotas are applied. Cutoffs expand.

Upward Movement Upgrades

Post physical college reporting.

Cutoffs drop 15%–35% as students withdraw for NITs/IIITs. Your allotted seat remains 100% safe if un-upgraded!

Special Spot Round

Decentralized physical counselling.

Conducted directly at college campuses for leftover seats. Cutoffs drop to lowest qualifying eligibility (45% PCM).

REAP 2027 Cutoff Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, authoritative answers to the most common queries regarding closing ranks, percentiles, TFWS cutoffs, and upward movement relaxation.

REAP cutoffs are generated dynamically at the conclusion of each seat allotment round by the centralized allocation algorithm managed by the Centre for Electronic Governance (CEG), Jaipur. Cutoffs represent the lowest merit position (either JEE Main All India CRL or 12th Board PCM %) that secured admission into a specific branch at a particular engineering college under the respective category and quota.

For General Rajasthan Domicile candidates, securing a Government-Aided (GAS) seat in Computer Science at MBM University Jodhpur typically requires a JEE Main All India CRL rank below 70,000 (approx. 94.5+ percentile). For a Self-Financed (SFS) seat in CSE, the cutoff relaxes to around 95,000 CRL (approx. 92.5–93 percentile). Candidates belonging to OBC, EWS, SC, or ST receive further relaxation.

Yes, under Priority 2. However, because JEE Main CRL holders have absolute Priority 1, high-demand branches like CSE and AI/DS in MBM and CTAE are usually filled by JEE candidates. Priority 2 candidates with 12th PCM percentages above 88%–92% frequently secure core engineering branches (Mechanical, Civil, Mining, Agricultural, Electrical) or CSE in top RTU-affiliated private institutions like SKIT and JECRC.

TFWS offers 100% waiver of tuition fees across all 4 years of engineering. Under AICTE and state norms, TFWS seats are limited to only 5% supernumerary seats per branch (e.g., only 3 seats in a branch with 60 intake). High-scoring meritorious students whose family income is below ₹8.00 Lakhs compete intensely for these limited zero-tuition seats, pushing closing ranks 15,000 to 30,000 ranks higher than General GAS seats.

No! Upward Movement is 100% zero-risk. If you physically report to your allotted college, deposit initial fees, and opt for Upward Movement, your current seat remains 100% reserved. If a higher choice on your locked list becomes available due to seat vacations, you are upgraded and your previous seat is released. If no higher preference can be allotted, your original seat remains completely safe and guaranteed.

The Government of Rajasthan mandates a 25% horizontal reservation for female candidates across all vertical categories (GEN, SC, ST, OBC, MBC, EWS). This ensures that one in every four seats in every branch is reserved for women, resulting in relaxed closing ranks typically 8,000 to 25,000 ranks deeper than male candidates in the same category.

After centralized Upward Movement concludes, individual institutions publish lists of remaining vacant seats and conduct decentralized physical Spot Rounds at college campuses. During Spot Rounds, standard merit cutoffs drop drastically, and admission is granted strictly on a first-come, first-served merit basis among attending registered candidates meeting minimum eligibility criteria (45% PCM General, 40% PCM Reserved).
