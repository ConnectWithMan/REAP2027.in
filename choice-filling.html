Official Choice Filling & Locking Regulations

Standard operating protocol enforced by the Centre for Electronic Governance (CEG), Rajasthan for centralized engineering seat distribution.

01

Top-Down Allocation Engine

The seat allotment engine checks your choices in strict numerical order: Preference #1 first, followed by #2, #3, etc.

  • The moment an open seat matches your merit rank and category, the algorithm allocates it immediately.
  • The system never skips to a lower preference if an upper choice is available.
02

Single Window Choice Economy

Online choice filling takes place exclusively in one dedicated session before Round 1 seat allotment declarations.

  • No second choice-filling session is conducted for subsequent rounds or upward movement.
  • A candidate who does not submit choices in this window is excluded from centralized counseling.
03

OTP Authentication Protocol

Final choice submission requires cryptographic confirmation via an OTP dispatched to your registered mobile number.

  • Manual locking requires entering the valid 6-digit OTP.
  • If left unlocked, your last saved draft auto-locks at 11:59 PM on the final deadline.
04

Upward Movement Boundary Rule

If you accept your allotted seat and apply for Upward Movement, the system only evaluates preferences above your allotted seat.

  • Example: If allotted Choice #7, upward movement only scans Choices #1 through #6.
  • Choices #8, #9, and beyond are completely erased from consideration.
05

GAS vs SFS Preference Weight

Government colleges offer both Government Aided (GAS) and Self-Financed (SFS) seats for the same engineering branch.

  • GAS seats have tuition fees under ₹30,000/yr; SFS seats cost ~₹75,000/yr.
  • Always order GAS above SFS for identical branches to prevent unnecessary financial liability.
06

TFWS Supernumerary Seat Rules

Candidates with valid parental income certificate (< ₹8.0 LPA) can opt for Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS) choices.

  • TFWS choices are designated with a separate -TFWS branch suffix.
  • Listing TFWS does not prevent you from adding normal GAS/SFS choices as backup.

Step-by-Step Online Choice Filling Procedure

Follow this systematic roadmap to navigate the CEG Rajasthan candidate portal from initial login to locked receipt generation.

1

Candidate Authentication & Verification

Access the candidate portal via your assigned REAP 2027 Application Number and secret password. Verify that your ₹885 non-refundable registration fee status is marked "Fee Paid / Verified" and that your Priority Category (Priority 1: JEE Main CRL, Priority 2: 12th PCM Percentile, Priority 3: Diploma) matches your original registration slip.

  • Confirm registered mobile number for OTP reception
  • Verify domicile status (Rajasthan vs Outside Rajasthan)
2

Access Choice Filling Console

On your main candidate dashboard, click on "Fill College & Branch Choices". The portal loads the centralized master directory of all CEG-affiliated engineering colleges, RTU constituent colleges, autonomous universities (MBM Jodhpur, CTAE Udaipur), and private universities.

  • Filter by District (Jaipur, Jodhpur, Udaipur, Kota, Bikaner)
  • Filter by College Type (Govt / Autonomous / Private)
3

Select Colleges, Branches & Seat Types

Add college-branch choices into your active list using the "+" button. Pay meticulous attention to the 3-digit official CEG Institute Code and the seat category tag: GAS, SFS, or TFWS.

  • Ensure you do not confuse campus codes (e.g. 001 MBM vs 002 CTAE)
  • Add both emerging tech (AI-DS, CSE) and core branches as backup
4

Reorder & Prioritize Preferences

Reorganize your preferences using the Move Up, Move Down, or Direct Position Reassignment tools. Ensure that Preference #1 is your absolute dream choice, regardless of your rank. The system will never penalize you for listing an ambitious college first.

  • Check that TFWS is placed above SFS for each college
  • Keep high-interest branches above low-interest branches
5

Save Draft & Inspect PDF Preview

Click "Save Draft Choices" frequently. Generate and download the Draft Choice Preview PDF. Print or thoroughly inspect this document with your parents and mentors to verify spelling, branch names, and institute ordering before committing to permanent locking.

  • Cross-check at least 30 to 80 choices
  • Verify that no unwanted private colleges exist in top 20
6

OTP Authentication & Final Locking

Once fully satisfied, click "Lock Choices". Enter the 6-digit OTP sent to your mobile number. Upon successful verification, the screen displays a green success confirmation. Download and print 3 physical copies of your Locked Choice Acknowledgement Form bearing your cryptographic submission timestamp and IP stamp.

  • Retain locked choice PDF for physical reporting verification
  • System locks permanently; no changes allowed thereafter

6 Fatal Choice Filling Mistakes Candidates Make

Every year, hundreds of students with outstanding JEE ranks lose top college seats due to easily preventable errors.

Mistake 1: Submitting Only 5 to 10 Choices

Believing that filling few choices forces the system to give you a dream seat is a myth. If your score misses those 5 cutoffs, you receive zero allotment in Round 1 and risk losing your admission momentum.

Fix: Fill at least 35 to 80 choices spanning Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3.

Mistake 2: Placing SFS Above GAS for Same College

Candidates often inadvertently click SFS before GAS. If the system finds an opening in SFS first, it immediately confirms the high-fee seat, wasting up to ₹2,50,000 over 4 years.

Fix: Audit your list so every GAS choice sits higher than its SFS twin.

Mistake 3: Choosing Unwanted Branches for "Campus Tag"

Adding Mining, Metallurgy, or Production in a top college when you only want to work in Software Engineering can trap you if internal branch sliding cutoffs do not drop.

Fix: Prioritize branch over brand if you have zero interest in core disciplines.

Mistake 4: Forgetting That Upward Movement is One-Way

If you list a college you dislike at Preference #5 and your favorite private college at Preference #6, and get allotted #5, Upward Movement will NEVER consider #6.

Fix: Strictly ensure your personal preference order matches numerical rankings.

Mistake 5: Not Verifying the 6-Digit SMS OTP

Leaving the session in draft status on the last day thinking it is locked can cause unexpected errors if the network drops before 11:59 PM auto-locking.

Fix: Manually lock choices 24 hours before deadline and download stamped receipt.

Mistake 6: Assuming Choices Can Be Re-entered in Round 2

CEG Rajasthan does NOT conduct a fresh choice filling window for Round 2. Round 2 allocations use the very same choices you locked in Round 1.

Fix: Treat this initial choice filling window as your only opportunity.
